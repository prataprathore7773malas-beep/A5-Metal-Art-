The A5 Story

Metal with a point of view.

At A5 Metal Art, metal isn't simply a material. It is a medium for expression, structure and timeless design.

We create distinctive cast iron and metal pieces for restaurants, cafés, residences, hospitality spaces, architects and designers who value craftsmanship that lasts.

Every curve, edge and intricate detail is made to give a space identity — combining traditional metal craftsmanship with contemporary aesthetics.
